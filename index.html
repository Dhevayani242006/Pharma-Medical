PharmaCare Management System

Pharmacy Billing, Customer Management & Stock Level Control powered by Express.js REST APIs and persistent Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`) data storage.

Excel (.xlsx) Database

Zero-database-server overhead. Uses SheetJS npm engine to persist stock, customers, sales, and line items directly into Excel worksheets.

POS Billing & UPI QR Code

Point-of-sale terminal with instant stock search, inline customer creation, automated stock deduction, UPI QR code scanner, and printable thermal receipt generator.

All Stocks Level Control

Automated stock level tracking with real-time indicators for In Stock, Low Stock, and Out of Stock thresholds.
